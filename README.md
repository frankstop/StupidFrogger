## How This Came to Exist

This game started with a short comedy video about a developer who sets out to make a simple *Frogger* game—just a frog hopping across a road—and gets completely carried away adding *"just one more feature"* until the frog is doing action-movie dodge rolls, roundhouse-kicking cars into the sky, backflipping across traffic, and paragliding over a river.

The game in the video didn't actually exist to play. So I handed the link to an AI with a single prompt.

### The Exact Prompt

```text
I want to play this game.  It does not yet exist.  Make it for me
https://youtube.com/shorts/F7y61toloEQ?si=5bZAmi3xSKAj4u5V
```

---

## How the AI Built It (Big Picture)

Without being told what was inside the video, the AI went from a raw link to a finished, playable 3D game in five big-picture steps:

1. **Watched the Video and Broke Down the Joke**  
   First, the AI opened the video and watched it frame by frame. It figured out the premise on its own: the game starts as classic 1980s *Frogger*, then suddenly shifts into an over-the-top 3D action game. It took note of the blocky retro look, how the camera swings down the highway, and every single move the frog performs from the starting lawn to the forest on the far side of the river.

2. **Captured the Original Sounds and Music Vibe**  
   To make the game *feel* like the video, the AI grabbed the actual sound effects from the clip—the 8-bit arcade tune at the start, the martial-arts grunts (*"Hup!"*, *"ORYAA!"*), the car crash, and the whoosh of the glider opening—and paired them with background music that automatically switches from retro arcade bleeps to dramatic boss-fight music and calm gliding melodies depending on what you're doing.

3. **Built the 3D World and the Frog from Scratch**  
   Next, it sculpted the entire scene in 3D to match the video's nostalgic, low-resolution style: the bright green grass, an eight-lane highway packed with colorful cars, a rushing blue river with floating logs, and a forest on the far shore. It also built an animated 3D frog with a thick cartoon outline and a fold-out green-and-white paraglider.

4. **Turned Every Visual Gag into Real Game Controls**  
   Instead of just making an animation, the AI turned every ridiculous moment from the clip into a move you can actually trigger at any time:
   - **Classic Hop:** Hop forward lane by lane like the original arcade game.
   - **Action Dodge Roll:** Dive and roll past speeding traffic like an action-RPG hero.
   - **Car-Launching Spin Kick:** Leap into the air and roundhouse-kick two-ton cars tumbling into the sky.
   - **360° Backflip:** Flip over traffic lanes or dive-flip off river logs onto dry land.
   - **Paraglider:** Pop open a glider mid-air to soar across the river from log to log.

5. **Recreated the Video as an Interactive Scene**  
   Finally, the AI choreographed the exact 28-second sequence from the original video right inside the game engine. You can press **Replay Video Scene** to watch the whole clip play out live in 3D—and press any movement key at any second to take over the frog yourself.

## Quick Controls

- **Move / Sprint:** `W` `A` `S` `D` or `Arrow Keys`
- **Orbit Camera / Switch Highway View:** Drag with mouse, `Q` / `E`, or press `C`
- **Jump / Deploy Paraglider in Air:** `Space` (or `G`)
- **Dodge Roll:** `Shift` or `R` (or `Right-Click`)
- **Roundhouse Kick a Car:** `F` or `K`
- **360° Acrobatic Flip:** `V` or `X`
- **Replay the Original Video Scene:** `P` *(or click the buttons along the bottom of the screen!)*
